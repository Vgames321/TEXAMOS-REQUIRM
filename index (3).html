    Rules

    Movement

    Click a square next to you to move there, or use the arrow keys. You can only move one square at a time, up, down, left, or right, never diagonally. Squares you can move to have a dashed outline. Your SPD (speed) is how fast you walk. Heavy gear slows you down.

    Enemy encounters

    Enemies wander around inside places. Beaten enemies vanish and come back after a while. Bosses stand still and guard the way, so you have to beat them to get past.

    Hiding and being chased

    Enemies can see you from a few squares away, but trees, houses, walls, and rocks block their view, so you can hide behind them. If an enemy only catches a glimpse of you, it gets curious (?), comes to look, and soon loses interest. If it's sure it saw you (!), it chases you. Hide, and it goes to the last place it saw you and searches for a while before giving up.

    Walk into an enemy that hasn't noticed you and you can sneak attack: you hit first (or leave it alone). If a chasing enemy catches up to you, the fight starts right away and it hits first. If you walk into one that's already chasing you, the fight starts right away and whoever has more speed hits first. Only sneak attacks, bosses that stand still, and bosses you already beat ask you first.

    Flash bombs (item shop), flash arrows (dropped by scouts and bandits), and the Blind spell (spell shop) blind enemies (✦) so they lose you. In a fight they stun the enemy so it skips turns. Use them on the map with the buttons in the stats bar, the keys F, R, and B, or Use in your Inventory. There's no running once an enemy catches you, so blind it (or hide) before it reaches you!

    Combat

    Combat is turn-based. You usually act first (Attack, Defend, or something from the Skills, Magic, or Items menus), then the enemy acts. (A chasing enemy that catches you, or one faster than you, acts first.) ATK is short for attack and HP is short for health points. If you die, your save file is deleted and you start over.

    MP in combat

    If an enemy has an ability and enough MP to use it, a 6-sided die is rolled every turn. If the roll matches the ability, the enemy uses it instead of its normal attack. The game rolls the dice for you.

    Fleeing

    Press Flee to escape a fight. You get a head start, but the enemy might chase you, so get out of sight!

    Hitting from a distance

    Bows and crossbows can shoot enemies on the map (4 squares or more), reach weapons like the longsword, extra longsword and halberd can hit a square or two past the one next to you, and stronger magic weapons can cast a bolt (5 MP). Click the enemy, or press E for the closest one. If it survives, it charges at you, and when it gets to you the fight starts: the faster one goes first. Bosses always survive the first hit.

    Archers and mages can do the same to you: they shoot you on the map, then come for you. A shot on the map never takes your last HP.

    Rooms, tunnels and floors

    Lots of places are more than one map now. Doors, gates, stairs, hatches and tunnels lead to other rooms: step on one to go through (it says where it goes). Some are locked until you beat the boss with the key. Look around: there are side rooms, tunnels and chests everywhere. Tunnels are dark.

    The road to the Imperial City is flooded, so the only way in is through the Deep Bog, every time.

    Enemy camps

    The Goblin base and the Bandit hideout are organized now. Warriors stay in the barracks, archers watch from their lookouts, mages guard their leader, and the treasure room (or vault) stays locked until you beat the boss who has the key. Enemies there stay close to their posts.

    After you save the city

    Once the true king is free, the empire's soldiers, guards, knights, paladins, mages, scouts and rangers are on your side (✓). They won't chase you, and you only fight one if you walk up to it and choose Attack. Soldiers also guard the villages.

    Raids can start in the villages and the Imperial City: a war horn sounds, then goblins (or bandits, in the city) march in, in waves, with a leader (♛). Shops bar their doors until it's over. The guards fight the small raiders, but the leader is yours to beat. Beat it and everyone who helped gets a reward. Take too long and the raiders steal a shop's goods: that shop stays empty until someone beats the leader at their base (the Goblin base's treasure room, or the Bandit hideout's vault).

    Online, you only see players who are at the same point in the story as you: before saving the city, or after.

    The story

    You start in your home village and work your way to the King of Hell. Your goal is always shown in the stats bar and the Journal. Story fights are fought alone.

    The world map

    The world map is for traveling. Step onto a place to go inside, and walk off its edge to leave. Mountains, ocean, and lava block you, and enemies can ambush you while you travel.

    Exploring

    Off the main road there’s a bandit hideout, a haunted crypt, a frozen peak, a sunken grotto, and something burning deep in the Darklands. Each one has a boss with a rare drop, and people with jobs for you. Relics are hidden around the world too: each one makes you stronger for good. The Journal keeps track of the places, bosses, and relics you’ve found.

    Levels

    Beating enemies gives you EXP. Every level up gives you more max HP, attack, and max MP, and heals you all the way.

    Shields

    Some enemies have a power shield (blocks physical hits) or a ward (blocks magic). The Greek letter is how strong it is: α blocks 25%, β 50%, γ 75%, and δ blocks everything. Some enemies always have one, and some raise one for a few turns. Magic goes right through power shields, and normal hits go right through wards. The rapier pierces power shields, and heavy weapons (greatsword, mace, axes, hammers) crack a shield down a level with every hit.

    Enemies with tricks

    Scouts, soldiers, royal guards, and bandit archers call nearby friends when they spot you. Some enemies call for help in a fight too, and you have to beat the helper right after. Guards hate thugs and monsters: if they see one, they forget about you and fight it, and thugs run from guards. Paladins shove their own allies out of the way to reach you, and they bless themselves with holy magic. Most enemies can drop something when you beat them.

    Shops

    Walk into a shop, then walk into the counter to talk to the shopkeeper. Buy shows how each item compares to what you’re using (▲ better, ▼ worse). You can sell things for half price, but not gear you’re wearing, spells, or relics.

    Group fights

    When another player joins your fight, it becomes a group fight: everyone, players and enemies, takes turns in order of speed, one at a time, and you see every move. Pick your move on your turn (you have 30 seconds, then you defend). Fights are loud: every extra player draws another nearby enemy in. Enemies go after whoever hurts them most, but they finish off anyone who's almost down. Pick which enemy to hit by clicking it.

    Playing together

    Other players show up on your map with their level. A ⚔ means they’re in a fight. Walk onto their square to help (if you’re within 5 levels of them), or to challenge them to a duel if they aren’t fighting. Every player who joins a fight makes the enemy stronger, and everyone shares its HP. Story fights are always solo. You can turn helping and duels off in Settings.

    Guilds

    Once you’ve beaten the story, you can start a guild or join one. See the Guilds page under Community for how it works.

    Mission guilds

    Once you reach the Imperial City, visit the guild square: the Weapons, Magic, Archer, and Armored guilds. Join one (only one at a time: switching starts you over at the bottom). Each rank has 2 missions: hunts, finding things (a ✦ marks the spot), and boss raids (a ★ marks the boss, and friends can join in). Rank up with the guild master for a title, a small boost, and gear or spells you can’t buy. Once the city is free, the bounty board opens, with WANTED players who win a lot of duels.

    Seeing and sneaking

    Enemies face the way they walk and only see in a cone in front of them (Enemy stats says how far and how wide). When an enemy spots you, the squares it can see turn red, so you know which way to get out of sight. Enemies that haven't seen you don't show anything, so watch which way they walk. Sneak up from the side or from behind! Big enemies trample smaller ones in their way. You can turn the red squares off in Settings. Some places, like the Deep Bog, are dark: you only see a little around you, and deep water slows you down.

    Staying active

    A new character that doesn’t reach level 3 within 2 days of starting is deleted. If you’re away for over 2 weeks, the first time you lose your guild spot, and the second time your whole character is wiped.

    Villages, the Kingdom, and shops

    Treasure chests and most enemies are inside places, not out on the world map. Inside, walk into a shop to go in. You can only buy each piece of gear or spell once, and you can carry up to 5 of each potion.

    Gear

    Weapon damage adds to your base attack. Some gear is special: slow gear makes you skip a turn after every attack, chainmail lets you hit twice, reach weapons can start a fight from a distance, the rapier pierces power shields, heavy weapons crack shields, and bows can fire special arrows that enemies drop. Basic arrows never run out.

    Bosses

    Some areas can’t be reached until you beat a boss.

    MP, aka magic points

    Magic can be used to attack, defend, or heal, but it costs MP, and using magic replaces your attack for the turn.

    Stats

    Your stats show how strong you are. They change when you take damage or get upgrades. You start with 100 HP, 5 attack, 5 speed, a maximum of 10 MP, and 0 gold.

    Items and consumables

    Consumables can be used in battle to give you the edge. You can carry up to 5 of each one.

    Guilds

    PvP

    To duel someone, walk onto their square and pick Challenge. They can say yes or no. Duels take turns: Attack, Defend, Items, and Magic. Whoever has more speed goes first.

    You can’t flee a duel. If you leave the game in the middle of one, the other player gets the win.

    Nobody dies in a duel. The loser drops to 1 HP, and the winner takes one random item from the loser’s bag (never gear they’re wearing, spells, or relics).

    Don’t want to duel? Turn off Allow PvP duel challenges in Settings.
