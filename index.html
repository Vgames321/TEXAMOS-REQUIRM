    Rules

    Movement

    Click a square next to you to move there, or use the arrow keys. You can only move one square at a time, up, down, left, or right, never diagonally. Squares you can move to have a dashed outline.

    Barriers

    You can’t walk onto houses, rocks, or trees; go around them. You can walk along wall tiles, but only if you climb on from a ladder tile first, and you climb back down from a ladder too.

    Enemy encounters

    Step onto a square with an enemy and the fight starts. You can’t walk past an enemy without fighting it or fleeing.

    Combat

    Combat is turn-based. You hit first, taking your damage off the enemy’s HP, then the enemy hits you. ATK is short for attack and HP is short for health points. If you die, your save file is deleted and you start over.

    MP in combat

    If an enemy has an ability and enough MP to use it, a 6-sided die is rolled every turn. If the roll matches the ability, the enemy uses it instead of its normal attack. The game rolls the dice for you.

    Fleeing

    Press Flee to escape a fight. You go back to the square you came from.

    Villages and shops

    Step onto a village or merchant stall to shop. You can only buy each weapon or piece of armor once.

    Bosses

    Some areas can’t be reached until you beat a boss.

    MP, aka magic points

    Magic can be used to attack, defend, or heal, but it costs MP, and using magic replaces your attack for the turn.

    Stats

    Your stats show how strong you are. They change when you take damage or get upgrades. You start with 100 HP, 1 attack, a maximum of 10 MP, and 0 gold.

    Items and consumables

    Consumables can be used in battle to give you the edge. You can carry up to 5 of each one.

    Saving

    The game saves by itself in this browser.

    Guilds

    Guilds are groups of players who fight together and can’t fight each other. Guild members can also attack other players as a team. You need at least 3 players to make a guild. Message the creator to register a guild, add a player, or join one.

    Registered guilds

    Guilds need multiplayer, so they aren’t playable in this version yet.

    PvP

    You move up, down, left, and right inside the game area. To fight, touch another player with your cursor. You must stay in the game area at all times while fighting, and you can’t flee PvP fights. Leaving or fleeing gets you banned for 3 days. If you beat someone, you get one item from their inventory.

    PvP needs multiplayer, so it isn’t playable in this version yet.
